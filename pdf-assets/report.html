Executive summary

A Manchester community this large deserves infrastructure to match

Made Running has 79.1k Instagram followers, BBC coverage, and collabs with Balenciaga, boohooMAN and Barry's Bootcamp. It does not have a database. Sessions are announced in DMs. Attendance is remembered by feel. Runners who quietly stop showing up are noticed only by accident.

That gap — between the scale of the community and the fragility of the tooling — is the risk and the opportunity. SutekWellness closes it: a bespoke two-module platform, purpose-built for Made Running, that lives on your balance sheet rather than a succession of monthly SaaS invoices. It replaces Klaviyo, Circle, Eventbrite, HubSpot and Pipedrive simultaneously — and delivers one capability none of them can: automatic re-engagement when any runner goes thirty days without a session, surfacing a caring prompt so nobody slips away unnoticed.

That is "No One Gets Left Behind" expressed as software.

Own it. Don't rent it.

Rented SaaS is an operating expense. Bespoke software is a balance-sheet asset. The moment you stop paying Klaviyo, the lists vanish. The moment you stop paying Pipedrive, the pipeline disappears. SutekWellness is yours — and its value compounds the day you sell.

The old way
Rented tools, fragmented data, no safety net
  • Instagram DMs for session RSVPs — no searchable record
  • Spreadsheets to track who turned up — manually maintained
  • Klaviyo for email — $$$ / month; cancel and lose everything
  • Circle or Mighty Networks for community — another monthly fee
  • Eventbrite or RunSignup for sessions — another fee per ticket
  • HubSpot or Pipedrive for brand collabs — another subscription
  • No re-engagement logic — lapsed runners disappear silently
The new way
One platform, fully owned, compounding in value
  • Runners database with profile, history, status — always yours
  • Attendance tracked automatically, per session, per chapter
  • At-risk flag fires at 30 days — re-engagement prompt surfaces
  • Hub, workshops and volunteers in the same system
  • Collabs pipeline: press, brands, charity, sponsors — one view
  • Zero SaaS licence fees once built — you own the code
  • Valuation uplift: the system is an asset when you sell
Every SaaS subscription you cancel is revenue someone else was building on your back. SutekWellness puts that value on your balance sheet.

05

How the re-engagement engine works

Three steps, automatic. No manual auditing of spreadsheets required.

STEP 01
Session attendance logged
Each session is checked off in SutekWellness. Attendance syncs to every runner's profile instantly.
→
STEP 02
30-day clock starts
The system monitors the gap since each runner's last session. At 30 days, status shifts to At Risk automatically.
→
STEP 03
Caring prompt surfaces
The runner's name and context appear in the team's queue. A personal DM goes out. No one gets left behind.
